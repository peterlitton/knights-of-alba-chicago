7 May 2026

Seventh Chapter

University Club of Chicago

The Chicago Chapter convened its seventh Chapter on May 7, 2026, at the University Club of Chicago.

Among those present were Paola Di Favori, Italian Trade Commissioner in Chicago, and Robert Allegrini, President & CEO of the National Italian American Foundation.

Their participation continued a tradition established from the Chapter’s earliest gatherings of welcoming representatives of institutions closely connected with Italy and the Italian-American community.

27 October 2025

Sixth Chapter

Il Milanese, Chicago

The sixth Chapter was held on October 27, 2025, at Il Milanese in Chicago.

The Chapter welcomed Mario Bartoli, Counsel General of the Italian Consulate of Italy–Chicago, and Marco Verna, Italian Trade Commissioner, both of whom were present for the gathering.

Their participation continued the Chapter’s ongoing relationship with Italy’s diplomatic and trade representatives in Chicago.

24 March 2025

Fifth Chapter

University of Chicago

The Chicago Chapter held its fifth Chapter on March 24, 2025, at the University of Chicago.

During the gathering, Mario Bartoli, Counsel General of the Italian Consulate of Italy–Chicago, was inducted as an Honorary Knight.

Also present were Marco Verna, Italian Trade Commissioner, and Knight Robert Allegrini, continuing the participation of important Italian and Italian-American institutional leaders in the Chapter’s activities.

20 October 2024

Extraordinary Chapter

University Club of Chicago

An Extraordinary Chapter was held on October 20, 2024, at the University Club of Chicago in support of Italy’s petition to UNESCO for designation and protection of Italian Cuisine.

The occasion brought together an exceptional group of representatives from Italian government, diplomacy, trade, and cultural institutions.

Italian Agricultural Minister Francesco Lolabrigida, ICI President Matteo Zappos, and Veronafiere President Federico Bricolo were each inducted as Honorary Knights.

Also present were Mariangela Zappia, Italian Ambassador to the United States; Mario Bartoli, Counsel General of the Italian Consulate of Italy–Chicago; Marco Verna, Italian Trade Commissioner; and Knight Robert Allegrini, together with numerous other Italian dignitaries.

The Extraordinary Chapter represented a significant moment in the Chicago Chapter’s engagement with Italian institutions and in its support for the international recognition of Italy’s culinary heritage.

6 May 2024

Gioia Ristorante

The Chicago Chapter gathered for its fourth Chapter on May 6, 2024, at Gioia Ristorante.

Among the evening’s institutional guests was Marco Verna, Italian Trade Commissioner, continuing the relationship between the Chapter and Italy’s trade representation in Chicago.

14 November 2023

Third Chapter

University Club of Chicago

The Chicago Chapter’s third gathering was held on November 14, 2023, at the University Club of Chicago as part of the official program of the Week of Italian Cuisine in the World.

The evening included a white truffle masterclass, connecting one of Alba’s most celebrated products with the broader international celebration of Italian culinary culture.

Luca DiVita, Head of the Italian Cultural Institute, was inducted during the Chapter. Counsel General Thomas Botzios was present, as was Marco Verna, Italian Trade Commissioner, attending as a guest. Robert Allegrini also participated as a Knight.

The event further strengthened the Chapter’s relationship with Italy’s diplomatic, cultural, and trade institutions in Chicago.

12 September 2023

Second Chapter

Formento’s Ristorante

The Chicago Chapter convened its second gathering on September 12, 2023, at Formento’s Ristorante.

The Chapter was joined by Thomas Botzios, Counsel General of the Italian Consulate of Italy–Chicago, and Robert Allegrini, President of the National Italian American Foundation, both continuing their participation in the developing activities of the Chicago delegation.

9 May 2023

Inaugural Chapter

Exmoor Country Club

The Chicago Chapter held its inaugural gathering on May 9, 2023, at Exmoor Country Club, formally beginning the Chapter’s activities in Chicago.

Among those participating was Thomas Botzios, Counsel General of the Italian Consulate of Italy–Chicago, who was invested as an Honorary Knight. Robert Allegrini, President of the National Italian American Foundation, was also invested as a Knight.

The occasion established an early connection between the Chicago Chapter and leaders representing Italy and the Italian-American community in the United States.

The Chicago Delegation of the Ordine dei Cavalieri del Tartufo e dei Vini d'Alba — a food and wine fellowship established in Piedmont in 1967, brought to Chicago in 2023.
